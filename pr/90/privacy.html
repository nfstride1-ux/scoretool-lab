Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

ScoreTool is run by Nathaniel Stride (Archery Calendar), Western Australia. Contact: nfshold@gmail.com.

Kept on your phone

Your scores, plots, sight marks, setups, diary, photos and settings are stored on your phone. You don't need an account for the free app. A backup file is saved only where you choose.

What leaves your phone, and when

  • Leagues, match play and group scoring (including Lingkana): your display name and scores are shared with the other archers in that league or match through a public relay (ntfy.sh).
  • Archery Calendar link (optional): if you link it, the rounds you send and their photos are stored in your Archery Calendar account (Supabase, hosted by Supabase Inc.).
  • Coaching (Plus or Pro): if you tap Share my data with coach, the person who opens the link can see your scores, plots, analysis and diary, and can send you notes. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • Paid plans: you sign in with Google or Apple. ScoreTool keeps only your email address, so it can match your plan. Payments are handled by our payment processor. ScoreTool never sees or stores your card details. Your plan, its status and its end date are stored with your email.
  • Usage analytics: Microsoft Clarity records how the app is used (taps and scrolling) so it can be improved. Anything you type is hidden.
  • Feedback: messages you send from Help are emailed to us through FormSubmit.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. You can ask us to delete your plan record or Archery Calendar data at any time by emailing us. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes what's on your phone.
